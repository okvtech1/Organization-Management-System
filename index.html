Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there really a free trial?
Yes — every new account gets full access for 7 days, no card required. You'll see a countdown right on your dashboard, with a simple way to upgrade before or after it ends.
Does it really work without internet?
Yes. Every record you add is saved to your device immediately. Once you're back online, it syncs automatically — or you can tap "Sync Now" any time.
What devices can I use it on?
Any laptop, tablet, or phone — Windows, Mac, Android, or iOS. Install it right from your browser; no app store needed.
Can I bring in my existing spreadsheets?
Yes — every module has a downloadable CSV/JSON template with the exact columns expected, so importing your existing data is straightforward.
How many team members can I add?
That depends on your plan and tier — see the Pricing page for exact limits. You can activate, suspend, or remove teammates at any time from your dashboard.
Is my organization's data visible to anyone else?
No. Every organization's data is fully isolated. Team members only see their own records unless they're an Admin, who sees everything within their own organization only.
What if it's not right for us?
You're covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee on any paid plan — and you can cancel any time.
