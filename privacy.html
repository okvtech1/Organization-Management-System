1. Introduction

OKV Technology Consults ("OKV Technology," "we," "us," or "our") builds and operates the OKV Organization Management System ("OKV OMS," or the "Service") — a member, finance, and operations management application used by organizations across Nigeria and beyond. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices and rights available to you.

By creating an account, using the Service, or visiting our website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Service.

2. Who This Applies To

OKV OMS is a multi-tenant system used by "Organizations" (churches, schools, SACCOs, NGOs, businesses, and similar bodies) to manage their own members, staff, donors, finances, and programs. Two categories of people interact with the Service, and this Policy covers both:

  • Organization Admins and Users — the people who sign up for and log into OKV OMS on behalf of an Organization.
  • An Organization's own data subjects — the members, donors, staff, volunteers, or beneficiaries an Organization enters into the system while using it.

For account, billing, and platform-usage data, OKV Technology acts as a data controller. For the records an Organization enters about its own members, donors, staff, or beneficiaries, OKV Technology acts only as a data processor — the Organization itself is the controller of that data and is responsible for having a lawful basis (such as consent) to collect and process it. If you are one of an Organization's members and have questions about your personal data held in OKV OMS, please contact that Organization directly; we will support them in fulfilling your request.

3. Information We Collect

Account & billing information — organization name, admin full name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text), selected plan and billing cycle, and payment/transaction references from our payment partners.

Organization data — whatever an Organization chooses to enter into its modules (Members, Staff, Finance, Events, Projects, Donors, Assets, Attendance, Outreach, Volunteers, Fees, Departments). This can include names, contact details, financial transactions, attendance records, and similar operational data about that Organization's own people.

Usage & device data — pages visited, actions taken within the app, browser/device type, and approximate access times, used to keep the Service reliable and secure.

Communications — messages you send us (e.g. support emails), and platform messages we send to your Organization (trial/subscription reminders, payment confirmations, service notices).

Local device storage — OKV OMS is built to work offline. A copy of your Organization's data is cached in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) so the app keeps working without an internet connection, and syncs back to your Organization's spreadsheet once you're back online.

4. How We Use Information

  • To create and operate your account and your Organization's dedicated workspace.
  • To provide the core features of the Service — records management, reporting, offline sync, messaging, and billing.
  • To process trial and subscription payments and send related receipts/confirmations.
  • To send service, security, and billing-related communications (e.g. trial-expiry reminders).
  • To monitor system health — including per-organization storage/capacity monitoring — and improve reliability and performance.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use Organization data entered into the app (member/donor/staff records) for advertising.

5. Our Legal Basis for Processing

Where the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("NDPA") or similar data protection law applies, we process personal data on one or more of these bases: performance of a contract (delivering the Service you signed up for), consent (e.g. at signup, or for optional communication channels), legitimate interest (e.g. keeping the Service secure and functioning), and compliance with legal obligations.

6. How Data Is Stored & Secured

OKV OMS is built on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script. Each Organization is given its own dedicated, private Google Spreadsheet for its records — Organization data is never pooled into one shared file with other Organizations. A separate, small master spreadsheet holds only account/billing metadata (never Organization transactional records).

Access to the backend is gated through authenticated API calls; passwords are stored as salted hashes, not plain text; and all traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Offline data cached on your device is stored in your browser's local storage and is only as secure as the device it's stored on — we recommend using a device with a passcode/lock screen, especially on shared computers.

No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable, industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect the data entrusted to us.

7. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell personal data. We share data only as necessary to operate the Service, with the following categories of service providers, and only to the extent your Organization has enabled the relevant feature:

  • Google (Workspace/Cloud) — hosts the Sheets/Apps Script infrastructure the Service runs on.
  • Paystack and/or Flutterwave — process card and bank payments for subscription upgrades, if your Organization pays online.
  • Termii or Twilio — deliver SMS messages, only if your Organization's Super Admin has enabled real SMS sending.
  • WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta) — delivers WhatsApp messages, only if enabled.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice to affected users where required).

8. Data Retention

We retain account and Organization data for as long as the Organization's account remains active, plus a reasonable period afterward to allow for reactivation, comply with legal/accounting obligations, and resolve disputes. An Organization Admin may request export or deletion of their Organization's data at any time — see Section 9.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your role and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we (or, for Organization-entered records, your Organization) hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request export of your data (Admins can export any module to CSV/JSON directly from the dashboard).
  • Request deletion of your account or data, subject to legal/record-keeping obligations.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing already carried out.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights over your own account/billing data, contact us using the details in Section 14. If your data was entered by an Organization you belong to (e.g. as a member, donor, or staff record), please contact that Organization directly — we will assist them as needed.

10. Cookies & Local Storage

Our marketing website uses minimal cookies/local storage for essential functionality (e.g. remembering that you've logged in). The app itself uses your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) to cache your Organization's data for offline use — this stays on your device and syncs to your Organization's spreadsheet, it is not a third-party tracking cookie. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

11. Children's Privacy

OKV OMS account holders (Organization Admins/Users) must be adults capable of entering into a binding agreement. Some Organizations (e.g. schools) may enter records relating to minors as part of their own legitimate operations (e.g. a student roster) — this is the Organization's responsibility as data controller, and Organizations must ensure they have appropriate legal grounds (such as parental/guardian consent, where required) before entering such data.

12. International Data Transfers

Because our infrastructure runs on Google's global cloud, data may be processed on servers located outside Nigeria. Where this occurs, we rely on our providers' recognized safeguards and standard contractual protections for cross-border data transfer.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Service or legal requirements. We will update the "Last updated" date above, and where changes are material, we will make reasonable efforts to notify Organization Admins by email.
