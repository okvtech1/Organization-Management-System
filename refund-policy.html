In short: Every new Organization gets a 7-day free trial with no card required, so you can try OKV OMS risk-free before paying anything. If you do pay for a plan and it isn't right for you, contact us within 30 days of that payment for a full refund — see Section 2 for the details.

1. Free Trial — No Payment Needed

Every new Organization automatically receives a 7-day free trial with full access to core features, and no payment method is required to start. This is the primary way we intend for you to evaluate whether OKV OMS is right for your Organization before spending anything.

2. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you make a paid subscription payment (via Paystack or manual bank transfer) and are not satisfied, you may request a full refund within 30 days of that payment, for any reason, provided the request is made in good faith. To request one, see Section 4.

3. What's Not Refundable

  • Requests made more than 30 days after the payment date.
  • Renewal payments for a subsequent billing cycle where the prior cycle was used substantially in full, unless required by applicable law.
  • Any portion of a billing cycle already used, beyond the 30-day window above (we don't pro-rate mid-cycle cancellations outside that window).
  • Payment processor fees actually incurred and non-recoverable from Paystack/Flutterwave, where applicable — these are deducted from an approved refund where the processor does not return them to us.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email us at the address in Section 12 with your Organization name, the email address used to sign up, the approximate payment date, and the reason for your request. We may ask a couple of clarifying questions, but we aim to keep this simple — there's no lengthy form to fill out.

5. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days, though your bank or card issuer may take longer to reflect it on your statement. Refunds for manual bank transfer payments are sent back to the bank account the payment originated from.

6. Cancelling Your Subscription

There is no lock-in contract. Subscriptions are prepaid for a billing cycle and do not auto-renew — simply choose not to make another payment when your current cycle ends, and your Organization's access will move to a read-only/limited state at expiry rather than being charged automatically. You can also let us know in writing at any time that you don't intend to renew, and we'll note it on your account.

7. Downgrades & Plan Changes

You can switch plans (e.g. Starter to Growth, or monthly to yearly) starting from your next billing cycle. We don't automatically refund the difference for a mid-cycle downgrade; if you'd like to discuss a mid-cycle change, contact us and we'll work it out with you directly.

8. Manual Bank Transfer Payments

If your Organization pays by manual bank transfer, your payment is reviewed and confirmed by our team before your plan is upgraded — refund requests for these follow the same 30-day window in Section 2, calculated from the date we confirmed (not the date you initiated) the transfer.

9. Failed or Duplicate Charges

If a payment fails but funds were still deducted from your account, or if you were accidentally charged twice for the same billing cycle, contact us immediately — this is always fully refundable regardless of the 30-day window, once we've verified it with our payment partner.

10. Chargebacks & Disputes

We'd genuinely rather resolve a billing concern directly — please reach out to us first using the details in Section 12 before filing a chargeback with your bank or card issuer. Chargebacks filed without first contacting us may result in suspension of your Organization's account while the dispute is investigated.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above; changes apply to payments made after the update, not retroactively to payments already made under a prior version of this Policy.
