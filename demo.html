Backup & RestorePro

Everything lives on this device only. Download a backup regularly, or restore from one — no internet needed.

Which format should I use?
JSON — one file, everything included (all 12 modules + your settings). This is the one to restore from — use it for real backups.
CSV — one spreadsheet-friendly file per module (Members.csv, Finance.csv, etc.), for opening in Excel/Sheets or importing elsewhere. Restore doesn't work from CSV — it's for viewing/editing outside the app, not backup-and-restore.

How to Renew

OKV Organization Management System offers three billing cycles — choose whichever suits your organization:

  • Monthly — billed every month, cancel any time.
  • Bi-Annual — billed every 6 months, saves 10% versus monthly.
  • Yearly — billed once a year, saves 20% versus monthly.

Click Upgrade / Renew Plan above to review plans and pricing, or contact us any time for help choosing the right one. Your team keeps using the app right up until your access expires.
